Privacy Policy

Keepfall: Castle Cannon Duel · Effective 8 October 2026

The short version: the game collects no data. There are no ads, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app stores on your device

Your profile (player name, level, trophies, streaks and statistics), achievements, campaign progress, castle designs, the cosmetics you own and have chosen, and settings are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the developer collects

Nothing. The game contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising code, and the developer runs no servers. No personal data, usage data or identifiers are sent to the developer or any third party.

Playing with other people

In online and nearby matches, your device sends the other players your player name, level, trophy count, castle design, the cosmetics you have chosen and your shots so the match can be played. Nearby matches connect devices directly over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth (Apple's Multipeer Connectivity); this is why the game asks for Local Network access. Castle codes you share contain only your castle design, and are sent only where you choose to share them.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game submits scores to leaderboards, reports achievements, and uses Game Center matchmaking for online matches. Match traffic goes through Apple's GameKit; the developer runs no servers. Your Game Center data is handled by Apple under your Game Center settings.

In-app purchases

Optional cosmetic purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. The game keeps a local note of what you own so it can show it, and asks Apple to restore it when you tap Restore. No receipt or payment information is sent anywhere by the game.

Tracking

The game does not track you across apps or websites, does not use the advertising identifier, and does not share data with data brokers or advertisers.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes any of the above, this page and the App Store privacy label will be updated first.
